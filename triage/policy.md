# Store policy

This is the policy of a fictional online store. It sells insulated bottles and ships to
Sweden, Finland, Norway, Denmark and the Netherlands.

## Delivery

- Orders are dispatched within 24 hours on business days.
- Delivery takes 3 to 6 business days in all five markets.
- A tracking link is sent by email when the order is dispatched.
- Shipping is free on orders of two bottles or more.

## Returns and exchanges

- Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- The customer pays the return shipping.
- The refund is paid within 5 business days after the return has arrived.
- One exchange per order is free of charge, for example to another size or colour.

## Damaged or wrong items

- The customer sends a photo of the item within 14 days of delivery.
- A replacement is sent free of charge. The customer does not need to send the item back.

## Changing or cancelling an order

- An order can be changed or cancelled until it has been dispatched.
- After dispatch it is handled as a normal return.

## Payment

- Card payments in all markets, Klarna in Sweden, Finland, Norway and Denmark, iDEAL in the Netherlands.
- Questions about a Klarna invoice, such as due dates and reminders, are handled by Klarna. The store can confirm the status of the order.

## Products

- The bottles come in 500 ml and 750 ml.
- They keep drinks cold for 24 hours and hot for 12 hours.
- The lid can go in the dishwasher. The bottle itself is washed by hand.
- All parts are BPA-free.
